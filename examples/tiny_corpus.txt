Darklearn is a small library for training neural networks with no
external dependencies. It starts with a tensor and a tape for gradients.
The tensor stores numbers in a flat array and every operation records
how to flow the gradient back. A linear layer is a matrix multiply plus
a bias, and an optimizer is a rule for moving weights downhill.
Training is a loop: forward, loss, backward, step. The loss falls a
little at a time until the model fits the data. The same loop trains
a tiny perceptron and a small language model, only the data changes.
The tokenizer turns text into numbers so the model can read it.
Byte pair encoding learns which pairs of bytes appear together most
often and merges them into single tokens. Common words become one
token, rare words stay as pieces. The model learns to predict the
next token from the tokens before it, one step at a time.
